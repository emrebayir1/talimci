# 🎓 Talimci

**Personalized Course Recommendation Assistant**

Talimci analyzes your learning goal, resume, and target job posting to
recommend the courses that fit you best.

### How it works
1. Answer a few quick questions about your learning goal (and, if you like,
   your resume, target job posting, and paid/free preference)
2. Talimci builds your profile and puts together a personalized course
   catalog for you
3. Get your first recommendations, then ask for as much detail as you want
   or request new suggestions anytime
